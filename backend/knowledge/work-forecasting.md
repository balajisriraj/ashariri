# The problem
Sample case study. A small retailer ordered stock from gut feel. Popular lines sold out every weekend and slow lines filled the stockroom.

# What I built
A weekly demand forecast per product and store, built from sales history, promotions and the calendar. The forecast fed a simple ordering screen that suggested quantities and explained each suggestion in one line.

# What I learned
The model mattered less than the screen. Store managers ignored forecasts they could not question, and adopted them once each number came with a reason and an easy way to override it.
