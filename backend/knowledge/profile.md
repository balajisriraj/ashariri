# Who I am
I'm Balaji Sri Raj, Manager, Forward Deployment Engineer at PSA International in Singapore. I build AI products people actually adopt. Twelve years turning messy operational problems - in ports, shipping, finance and HR - into AI that reaches production, and building the teams and capability to scale it.

# Quick facts
Agentic AI products that reach production. Highlights: a self-healing document intelligence product that most pilot business units chose to run, vessel tracking through the Suez and Red Sea crises, cost-to-serve pricing that won a group-wide challenge, and port-disruption early-warning agents. All 14 case studies are in the Work section.

Balaji has been building with LLMs since early 2023, starting with open-source models on in-house GPUs. Today that means multi-agent systems with tool use, memory, evals and human review: supervisor agents for finance, forward-deployed agent pods, and Balaji's own Lab products.

Balaji builds the product, then the people around it. Balaji founded the Enterprise AI team of forward-deployed engineers and the group's agentic working team, led the analytics graduate programme and ran a group AI challenge.

On strategy Balaji authored the group's AI governance framework, put AI spend in leaders' terms with a token-economics view, and wrote the AI tool-selection guide.

The Lab has ten side products. Three are under way: Ashariri (Balaji's digital twin, the one answering here), TikGenie for ticketing over WhatsApp, and FinGenie, a finance co-pilot on Tally Prime. Seven more are planned, each with its own GitHub repository.

Twelve-plus years: data analyst at Hi-Tech Titanium and Amazon, data scientist at Accenture, five and a half years at PSA Singapore, then PSA International, where Balaji is now Manager, Forward Deployment Engineer.

Three master's degrees: an MSc in Analytics from NTU Singapore, an MBA from SRM University and an MSc in Psychology from the University of Madras, plus a BSc in Computer Science.

Six wins at PSA: the group-wide data science challenge (2022), the global data story challenge (2023) and innovation awards in 2023, 2024 and twice in 2025.

Python, TypeScript and React; LangGraph, LangChain and agent SDKs; Claude and OpenAI models; Databricks, Azure AI Foundry, PostgreSQL and vector search; plus classic ML with PyTorch and scikit-learn.

Balaji's happy building at PSA, but always open to the right conversation, especially roles leading agentic AI products or forward-deployed AI teams. The quickest way to start one is email: balajisriraj@outlook.com.

Email is best: balajisriraj@outlook.com. Balaji's also on LinkedIn at linkedin.com/in/balajisrdu.

Balaji has spoken at the Data Innovation Summit APAC, been a guest on national radio about AI and the future of humanity, and talked to data science students. The recordings are in the Talks section.

Outside work: writing, a long reading list from ML to Tamil classics, talks, and the Lab. Balaji's day ends with reading before sleep.
