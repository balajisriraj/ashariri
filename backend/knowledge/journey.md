# From analyst to AI builder
Data, then models, then products, then agents and teams.

# Hi-Tech Titanium (2011 - 2012)
Data Analyst at Hi-Tech Titanium. Operational reports and executive dashboards. Where I learnt that a number only matters if someone acts on it.

# Amazon (2013)
Data Analyst, Digital at Amazon. Competitor benchmarking and pricing analytics, and support for catalogue launches across global locations.

# Accenture (2017 - 2019)
Data Scientist at Accenture. Six predictive and visual analytics solutions for global insurance and health clients, real-time sentiment models and security ML proofs of concept.

# PSA Singapore (2019 - 2024)
Senior Lead Data Scientist at PSA Singapore. Five and a half years inside the operating engine of the world's busiest transhipment hub: stowage, cranes and vessel arrival; then commercial analytics through two shipping crises; then the first wave of generative AI. Promoted in 2023.

# PSA International (Oct 2024 - Oct 2025)
Manager, Data & AI Excellence at PSA International. Moved from one business unit to the whole group: communications and competency lead for data and AI, and group AI products adopted by business units in other countries. Promoted to Manager in 2025.

# PSA International (Nov 2025 - Jul 2026)
Co-Head of Data Science, Group Data Office at PSA International. Scaled document intelligence across regions, ran the group AI challenge, supported network-coordination AI and founded the group's agentic working team.

# PSA International (Aug 2026 - now)
Manager, Forward Deployment Engineer at PSA International. Founding manager of the Enterprise AI team in Group Enterprise IT: forward-deployed engineers embedded with group functions, plus AI governance and FinOps.
