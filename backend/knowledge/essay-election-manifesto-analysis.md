title: "Scientific Analysis of Indian Election Manifestos"
url: "https://balajisriraj.com/writing/election-manifesto-analysis"
kind: "essay"
---

Two manifestos, several hundred pages, and a simple question: what do the words
themselves reveal once you stop reading them politically and start counting them?

The analysis compares term frequency, topic emphasis and sentiment across both
documents. It makes no claim about which set of promises is better - only about
what each party chose to spend its words on.
