# How I lead
Shipping AI is half the job. The other half is people. AI only scales when the organisation does. My principles: adoption over demos, hand over don't hold on, and teach the next team.

# Build teams
I set up the teams that put AI where the work is.

- Founding manager of the Enterprise AI team: forward-deployed engineers embedded with group functions
- Founded the group's agentic working team, with Databricks as a partner
- Led a lean product pod from proof of concept to production and a clean handover

# Grow capability
I teach organisations to do it without me.

- Led the group's analytics graduate programme, with cohorts from business units worldwide
- Ran the group AI challenge from regional screening to a grand finale and innovation funding
- Trained teams on site in Türkiye and Saudi Arabia, and organised a group-wide speaker series on data

# Shape strategy
I help leaders decide what to build, buy and govern.

- Authored the group's AI governance framework
- Put AI spend in leaders' terms with a token-economics view and a case for local inference
- Wrote the AI tool-selection guide and contributed to the group's digital product blueprint
