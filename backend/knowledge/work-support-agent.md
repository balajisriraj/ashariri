# The problem
Sample case study. The support team answered the same few dozen questions every day, and new starters took weeks to learn where the answers lived. Customers waited, and the team spent its time copying from old tickets.

# What I built
A support agent that reads each new ticket, searches the help centre and past resolved tickets, and drafts a reply with citations. A person reviews every draft and sends it, edits it or throws it away. The agent never replies on its own.

# How it works
Hybrid retrieval over help articles and resolved tickets, a drafting step that must cite its sources, and a checker that blocks drafts that promise refunds or dates the sources do not support. Every edit the team makes is logged, and those edits become new eval cases.

# What changed
Reply times dropped, new starters became useful in days instead of weeks, and the team trusted the drafts because they could always see where an answer came from.
