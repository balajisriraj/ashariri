# Overview
Self-healing document intelligence (PSA International, 2025 - 26). LLM extraction that replaced a legacy document tool - and that most pilot business units chose to run in production. My role: Product owner and technical lead. Recognition: Innovation award ’25.

# The problem
Business units across the group were re-keying invoices, customs and logistics documents - often scanned, often multilingual. The legacy tool was expensive per page and needed dozens of samples for every new layout.

# What we built
A product on layout-aware OCR and large language models that extracts structured data with no per-format training. A verifier checks each field, failures loop through a self-healing path, and only low-confidence documents reach a person. I set the direction, ran discovery with business units, wrote the business case and led a lean pod.

Stack: Azure Doc Intelligence, OpenAI, Claude, Databricks, Python, React.

# Impact
- Piloted with business units across Asia, Europe and Central Asia
- Most pilot units chose to take it to production
- A fraction of the legacy cost per page, with no per-layout training
- Handed over to the business units to run themselves
