# Agent or chatbot
Sample essay. A chatbot answers. An agent decides what to do next: it can choose a tool, look something up, check its own work and try again. The difference is not the model, it is the loop around it.

# The loop is the product
I think the interesting engineering in an agent is the loop: how it routes a request, what it is allowed to call, how it knows when it is wrong and when it should stop and hand over to a person.

# Keep a human in the loop
I believe the best agents today draft and a person decides. That keeps the speed of automation and the judgement of the people who own the outcome.
