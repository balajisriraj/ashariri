# Overview
Vessel tracking through two shipping crises (PSA Singapore, 2021 - 24). Born during the Suez blockage, it became planners' live view of Singapore-bound vessels through the Red Sea disruption. My role: Product owner and lead developer.

# The problem
When the Suez Canal was blocked, planners needed to know where vessels bound for Singapore actually were - and the answer was spread across disconnected systems.

# What we built
A live tracking product that joins satellite AIS positions, port-community data, berth applications and arrival predictions into one production data table, with a cloud dashboard planners used for rapid impact analysis. I also ran the AIS data-vendor evaluation and hourly quality checks.

Stack: Python, Spark, Azure ML, Power BI, AIS data.

# Impact
- Carried the planning team through the Red Sea disruption
- Replaced recurring manual vessel searches every quarter
- Left a reusable production data asset for analysts
