# Overview
Generative AI, before it was easy (PSA Singapore, 2023). Open-source LLMs on in-house GPUs and retrieval chatbots built from scratch - the foundation of everything agentic since. My role: Lead, from GPU setup to fine-tuning and demos. Recognition: Innovation award ’23.

# The problem
In early 2023 enterprise LLM tools weren't available yet, but operations and training teams were working through large volumes of manuals and procedures.

# What we built
Ran a large open-source model on internal GPUs, built retrieval Q&A over training material with QLoRA fine-tuning, and an operations chat assistant. Then took the story to a global internal audience and into an OpenAI prototype for vessel monitoring.

Stack: PyTorch, Hugging Face, LangChain, Streamlit, Python.

# Impact
- Innovation award for a chat assistant for operations
- Presented generative AI to a global internal audience
- The blueprint for later RAG and agentic products
