The problem looked like an optimisation and turned out to be a negotiation:
service levels and operating cost pull in opposite directions, and the model only
mattered once both sides trusted the same numbers.

Winning the group's data science challenge was the visible part. The
useful part was everything that made it survive contact with operations
afterwards.
