Spreadsheets didn't transform business by digitizing ledgers. They made entirely
new workflows possible.

AI might be at the same inflection point - powerful models, impressive demos,
real boardroom enthusiasm. But in most organizations it still feels
underwhelming. Not because the technology is overhyped, but because the
conditions around it aren't ready.

Three things are quietly holding it back:

- **Processes designed for a pre-AI era.** The workflow assumes a human
  bottleneck that no longer has to exist.
- **Performance signals that can't see real effort anymore.** When the work
  changes shape, the old measures stop measuring anything.
- **Invisible infrastructure nobody budgets for.** The unglamorous plumbing that
  decides whether a pilot ever becomes a product.

This is Part 1: the diagnosis. Part 2, the prescription, is still being written.
