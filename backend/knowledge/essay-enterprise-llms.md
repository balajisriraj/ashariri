Notes from a session with data leaders across several markets, comparing where
generative AI had actually earned its place against where it had merely been
deployed.

The pattern that held across every organisation in the room: the constraint was
almost never model capability. It was whether the surrounding process could
absorb a probabilistic answer, and whether anyone owned the outcome when it was
wrong.
