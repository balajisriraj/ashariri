# Overview
From a challenge win to a finance tool (PSA Singapore, 2022 - 24). A cost-to-serve pricing idea won a group-wide data science challenge - then became a production pipeline for Finance and Commercial. My role: Co-lead, prototype to production. Recognition: Challenge winner ’22.

# The problem
Customers were priced on averages, not on what it actually cost to serve them across berths, yard, cranes, prime movers, lashing and reefers.

# What we built
A cost-apportionment pipeline that splits operating cost down to each billing customer, with a machine-learning model to re-apportion yard shuffling. It won a group-wide challenge judged by the Group CEO and board directors; the harder part was turning it into a production tool and sharing the method with other regions.

Stack: Python, scikit-learn, Spark, Power BI, SQL.

# Impact
- Won the group-wide data science challenge, 2022
- Production runs feeding Finance and Commercial customer reviews
- Removed days of manual work each quarter
- Method extended to other regions in the group
