An announcement that moved a very large amount of public conversation in a very
short window - and a good excuse to point a sentiment pipeline at live data
rather than a tidy training set.

The interesting part was not the sentiment scores. It was how quickly the
vocabulary of the conversation shifted, and how badly a model trained on general
English handled a bilingual audience.
