# Overview
Port disruption intelligence (PSA International, 2026). Agents read live news and operational signals to forecast which ports could be disrupted in the next 7, 30 or 90 days. My role: Product lead and builder.

# The problem
Strikes, severe weather, congestion and geopolitical events hit ports with little warning. The signs often surfaced in the news long before they reached planning teams - by then, the options had narrowed.

# What we built
An agentic early-warning system on Databricks: scout agents read live news across regions, a context agent adds internal operating signals, horizon models score port impact at 7, 30 and 90 days, and an analyst agent explains each risk through two lenses - protecting operations, or offering customers alternative routing. Voice and multilingual access built in.

Stack: Databricks, LangGraph, Spark, MLflow, Python.

# Impact
- Early warning for disruptions of every kind, across three forecast horizons
- One engine, two lenses: operations and commercial
- Showcased to senior leadership
