# Overview
LLM talent matching for HR (PSA Singapore → International, 2023 - 26). Two generations of people-matching: résumé ranking for recruiters, then plain-language search over development plans. My role: Lead data scientist, then product owner. Recognition: Innovation awards ’24 ’25.

# The problem
Recruiters screened résumés by hand, and Group HR searched thousands of development-plan records with keywords to find people for overseas postings.

# What we built
First, a résumé-to-role engine using embeddings, hybrid search and OCR - a working prototype with a UI in under a week, then MVP testing with recruiters. Next, an LLM assistant that searches development plans in plain language and ranks candidates, taken through user testing and data-privacy review with Group HR.

Stack: OpenAI embeddings, LangChain, Azure AI Search, OpenCV, Python.

# Impact
- Innovation awards in 2024 and 2025
- Presented to the senior management council
- Use case carried into Group HR's AI pilot programme
